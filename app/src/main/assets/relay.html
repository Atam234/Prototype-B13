      Upload not going through?

      1. Keep this tab open — messages are cached on this page, so you can switch networks safely.
      2. If uploads fail with a network error, your phone is sending everything over the sender's Wi-Fi (which has no internet). Turn Wi-Fi off, make sure mobile data is on, then tap Retry Relay.
      3. After the upload succeeds, turn Wi-Fi back on, rejoin the sender's hotspot and tap Retry Relay so the sender's phone learns the message was delivered.
